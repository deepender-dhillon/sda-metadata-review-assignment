## Overall Progress

## Top Compliance Issues

Three issue types account for almost all pending items.

### 1. Missing data owner name (4 submissions)

**Departments:** Basic Education, Energy, Home, and Labour.

This is a governance field, not a technical one, and its absence suggests these submissions went out before a named officer was confirmed as data owner.

### 2. DPDP Classification Mismatches (4 submissions)

**Departments:** Health, Agriculture, Women & Child Development, and Food & Civil Supplies.

All submitted personal-data datasets classified as Public or left blank. The same error across four unrelated departments suggests unfamiliarity with the Public/Restricted distinction, not carelessness.

### 3. Incomplete Core Fields (6 submissions)

Issues include:

- Missing descriptions
- Missing record counts
- Non-standard date formats (commonly DD-MM-YYYY instead of YYYY-MM-DD)

This suggests departments are using their own internal templates rather than the SDA's.

> **Observation:** These point to a **template and orientation gap**, not individual negligence i.e. the same handful of errors recur across otherwise unrelated departments.

## Recommended Actions (Next Two Weeks)

### 1. Send Escalated Second Follow-Ups

Send escalated second follow-ups to the seven non-responsive departments, with a firm deadline and named contact for queries.

### 2. Hold Short Working Calls

Hold short working calls with departments that have responded but not yet sent a revision (Urban Development, Transport, Basic Education) as it is often faster than email thread.

### 3. Issue a One-Page DPDP Classification Guidance Note

Issue a one-page DPDP classification guidance note to all departments, since this error recurs across four unrelated departments and is better solved through clarified guidance than one-off corrections.
